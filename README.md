# 🏆 Minesweeper Game

Enjoy a classic game of Minesweeper right in your browser! Test your logic, avoid the mines, and challenge yourself to clear the board as fast as possible.

### 🎮 Play Now:

➡️ [Minesweeper Game](https://thang0226.github.io/Minesweeper)

## 📌 Features

✅ Classic Minesweeper gameplay  
✅ Multiple difficulty levels  
✅ Timer & highscore tracking

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Choose your difficulty level (Easy/Medium/Hard)
2. Click "Play" and enjoy the game
3. Try to beat the high scores
